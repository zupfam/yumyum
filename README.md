# YumYum

**YumYum** is a mobile-first marketing, discovery, and digital storefront solution for street food in Bangalore.

Before a customer decides *what* to eat or *where* to go for good street food, they browse YumYum. We aim to be the canonical source of visual food data for the next generation of foodies.

## The Gaps (What we solve)
We solve the "Invisibility Problem" of street food vendors and create a brand for them.

- NO discovery
- NO data/feedback
- NO delight tech
- NO/fragmented social content
- NO brand

## Core Product Pillars

### 1. Visual Storefront
- **Insta-Reel Style Menu:** Replaces traditional static text with a vertical, visual menu experience.
- **Delight Tech:** Includes physical QR standees and storefront branding.

### 2. Discovery Engine
- **Live Location:** Discovery and live location tracking powered by Google Maps.
- **Strategic Targeting:** Systematically targeting high-density areas (like Bangalore HSR/Indiranagar) to build critical mass, making it super easy for customers searching for the best street food.

### 3. Social, Content & Feedback
- **Vlogger Aggregation:** Micro food vloggers can join, link their content directly to the store's menu, and help vendors for money. We can create a small Influencer x Store gig platform.
- **Smart Integrations:** Integrates with Google My Business, Instagram, Swiggy/Zomato, and WhatsApp for review collection.
- **Data & Marketing:** Provides vendors with marketing creatives, content strategies, and data with gamified metrics.

*YumYum aims to become the visual content layer that Zomato/Swiggy lacks.*
